About us

A family hospital for mothers, babies and critical care

Swastik Hospital is a multi-storey hospital on Shamshabad Road in Kaveri Vihar Phase 1, near Shaheed Nagar, Tajganj. It is run by a doctor couple: Dr. Vandana Jain, obstetrician and gynaecologist, and Dr. Ankit Jain, anaesthesia and critical care specialist.

The hospital is open 24 hours for normal and caesarean deliveries, painless delivery, gynaecology, ultrasound and emergencies. Patients on Google most often mention the gynaecologist, the polite staff, the experienced doctors and the clean rooms and wards.

  • 24-hour emergency services
  • In-house pharmacy
  • Wheelchair-accessible entrance, seating and toilet
  • Free parking with accessible car park
  • Nursing room for mothers
  • Cards and mobile payments accepted

Services

Care from pregnancy to recovery

Our main services, with the Hindi name you can use when you call.

Normal & Caesarean Delivery

सामान्य व ऑपरेशन द्वारा प्रसव

Labour room and operation theatre ready 24 hours for normal deliveries and C-sections.

Painless Delivery

दर्द रहित (Painless) प्रसव

Epidural analgesia given by an anaesthesia specialist, so a normal delivery is possible without labour pain.

CTG / NST Monitoring

बच्चे की धड़कन व मूवमेंट जाँच

Cardiotocography and non-stress tests to check your baby’s heartbeat and movement.

Ultrasound

अल्ट्रासाउंड जाँच

In-house ultrasound for pregnancy scans and gynaecology check-ups.

Gynaecology & Laparoscopy

स्त्री रोग व दूरबीन सर्जरी

Treatment of women’s health problems, including laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery.

Infertility Care

निःसंतानता उपचार

Check-ups and treatment for couples trying to conceive, guided by an infertility specialist.

Critical Care

क्रिटिकल केयर

Close monitoring and treatment for seriously ill patients, led by a critical care specialist.

Anaesthesia & Pain Relief

एनेस्थीसिया व दर्द निवारण

Safe anaesthesia for operations and relief for acute and long-standing pain.

General Medicine

सामान्य चिकित्सा

Consultations with a consultant physician for common illnesses and long-term conditions.
